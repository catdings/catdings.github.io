# CATding Publisher: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 7 November 2026*

CATding Publisher (the "App") is a private tool operated by **Richardy Lobo Sapan** that uploads the CATding channel's own videos to the operator's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. This policy explains what data the App handles.

## 1. Whose data
The App only signs in to accounts owned by its operator. It does not collect data from any other person, and it has no public users or sign-ups. Its website (catdings.github.io) is a static page hosted on GitHub Pages: it sets no cookies and runs no analytics. GitHub may log visits under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## 2. What data the App receives and why

| Platform | Permissions requested | Data received | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube (Google) | `youtube.upload` | An OAuth access and refresh token | Uploading the operator's videos to the operator's channel |
| Instagram (Meta) | `instagram_basic`, `instagram_content_publish`, `pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `business_management` | The linked Instagram account ID, Facebook Page name, and a Page access token | Publishing the operator's Reels to the operator's Instagram account |
| TikTok | `user.info.basic`, `video.upload`, `video.publish` | An access and refresh token, plus basic profile info (open ID, display name) | Sending the operator's videos to the operator's TikTok drafts, or posting them |

The App does **not** read posts, messages, followers, comments or any other user's data.

## 3. Storage
- Tokens and account IDs are stored **only on the operator's own computer**, in a local folder that is excluded from version control.
- Nothing is sent to any server other than the official Google, Meta and TikTok APIs.
- No data is sold, shared, rented or used for advertising or profiling.

## 4. Retention and deletion
Tokens are kept only while the operator uses the App. To delete them:
- delete the App's local token files; and/or
- revoke the App's access in your Google Account (Security → Third-party access), Facebook/Instagram (Settings → Business integrations / Apps and websites) or TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).

Revoking access immediately stops the App from using the account.

## 5. Children
The App is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly process any child's data.

## 6. Google API Services
**CATding Publisher uses YouTube API Services.** Using it is subject to the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms). You can revoke its access to your Google account at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/connections>. Revoking also stops its access to YouTube data.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
